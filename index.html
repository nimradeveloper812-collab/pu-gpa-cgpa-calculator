2. Key Evaluation & Calculation Rules

  • Ceiling Rule for Fractions: A fraction of marks in a course is counted as '1' mark. E.g., 64.1 or 64.9 is counted and shown as 65. Total 71.4 is counted as 72.
  • Individual Courses Only: Letter grades are only used for individual courses, not for semester GPA or CGPA.
  • Semester GPA Formula: Multiply course GP by Credit Hours to obtain Weighted Grade Points. Divide sum of weighted points by total semester credit hours.
  • Direct CGPA Computation: CGPA is directly computed from individual courses GPs across all enrolled and evaluated semesters (∑ ci · GPi / ∑ ci) and not by averaging semester GPAs.
  • OPM (Obtained Percent Marks): OPM = ∑(ci · Marksi) / ∑ ci.

🎓 How to Use — PU GPA & CGPA Calculator

Follow these 4 simple steps to calculate your GPA & CGPA

Step 1
Select Your Semester
Click any semester button (e.g. Sem 1) from the navigation bar below the cards. You can switch between all 8 semesters anytime.
Step 2
Enter Your Marks
Type your percentage marks (0–100) in the Marks field for each subject. Course name is optional. Set credit hours if different from default (3 CH).
Step 3
View Your Semester GPA
Your Semester GPA card (top left) updates instantly as you type. It shows your GPA, OPM%, grade points, and promotion status — all calculated per PU rules.
Step 4
Calculate CGPA Across Semesters
In the Cumulative CGPA card (top right), check the semester chips (Sem 1, Sem 2…) you want to include. Your CGPA updates automatically.
Where to see your GPA?
After entering marks, look at the top-left blue card — "SEMESTER GPA". It shows your GPA for the currently selected semester instantly.
Where to see your CGPA?
Look at the top-right card — "CUMULATIVE CGPA". Tick the semester checkboxes at the bottom of that card to include whichever semesters you want — CGPA updates automatically.
Rounding rule: If you enter 71.4, the calculator shows 72 — following the PU ceiling rule (fractions count as 1 mark).
Component calculator: Click the 🧮 button next to the marks field to enter Mid-term, Assignment, and Final marks separately.
Click the "PU Example" button in the header to load a real PU statute example with 2 semesters of data.
