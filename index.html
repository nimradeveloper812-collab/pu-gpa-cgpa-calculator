How to Calculate GPA & CGPA at Punjab University

The University of the Punjab (PU), Lahore adheres to a standardized semester examination policy for all 4-year BS programs and 2-year Associate Degree Programs (ADP). Here is how numerical grades convert into Grade Points (GP) and cumulative averages:

1. Course Quality Points (QP)
Quality Points = Credit Hours × Course Grade Point (GP)

For example, earning an A (4.00 GP) in a 3 Credit Hour course yields 3 × 4.00 = 12.00 Quality Points.

2. Semester GPA Formula
Semester GPA = ∑ (Credit Hours × GP) ÷ Total Semester Credits

Sum the quality points of all registered courses in that semester and divide by the total registered credit hours.

3. Cumulative CGPA Formula
Degree CGPA = ∑ (All Course Credits × GP) ÷ Total Degree Credits

Important Rule: Under PU examination statutes, CGPA is directly computed from individual courses across all semesters, not by averaging semester GPAs.

4. Obtained Percentage Marks (OPM)
OPM (%) = ∑ (Credit Hours × Marks) ÷ Total Degree Credits

Weighted average percentage of marks obtained across all completed courses in the degree program.

Official PU Fraction Ceiling Rule (Section 5):

A fraction of marks in a course is counted as ‘1’ mark. For example, 64.1 or 64.9 marks are rounded up to 65 (Grade B-, 2.70 GP). A total of 71.4 is counted and recorded as 72 (Grade B, 3.00 GP). This calculator automatically implements this rule on every subject entry.

Punjab University Promotion, Probation & Drop Rules

Sections 12 and 13 of the Punjab University examination regulations govern promotion from one semester to the next:

First Semester Regulations

  • GPA ≥ 2.00: Promoted to 2nd semester in Good Standing.
  • 1.50 ≤ GPA < 2.00: Promoted to 2nd semester on 1st Academic Probation.
  • GPA < 1.50: Automatically Dropped from Rolls of the university.

2nd Semester Onwards Regulations

  • CGPA ≥ 2.00: Promoted to the next semester in Good Standing.
  • 1.70 ≤ CGPA < 2.00: Promoted on Academic Probation (maximum 2 probations allowed throughout the degree).
  • CGPA < 1.70: Automatically Dropped from Rolls.
  • Failing after 2 Probations: If a student fails to achieve 2.00 CGPA after consuming 2 probations, they are dropped.

Degree Award Requirement

For the conferment of an Associate Degree (2 years) or Bachelor of Science Degree (BS 4 years), the candidate must obtain a minimum CGPA of 2.00 out of 4.00.

Frequently Asked Questions (PU GPA FAQs)

How is GPA calculated at Punjab University (PU)?

Semester GPA is calculated by multiplying each course’s earned Grade Point (GP) by its credit hours to get Quality Points, summing all Quality Points, and dividing by total enrolled credit hours: GPA = ∑(Credit Hours × GP) ÷ ∑(Credit Hours).

What is the Punjab University fraction ceiling rounding rule?

Under official PU examination regulations, any fraction of marks obtained in a course is counted as 1 full mark. For example, 64.1 or 64.9 marks are rounded up to 65 (Grade B-, GP 2.70), and 71.4 marks are counted as 72 (Grade B, GP 3.00). Our calculator applies this ceiling rule automatically.

How is CGPA calculated from multiple semesters at PU?

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is computed directly from all evaluated courses across all enrolled semesters: CGPA = ∑(All Course Credits × Course GP) ÷ ∑(Total Evaluated Credits). PU examination statutes explicitly specify that CGPA is not calculated by simply averaging semester GPAs.

What is the minimum CGPA required to graduate from Punjab University?

A minimum cumulative CGPA of 2.00 out of 4.00 is strictly required for the award of both 4-year Bachelor’s degrees (BS) and 2-year Associate degrees (ADP) at Punjab University.

What happens if a student receives an 'F' or 'D' grade?

An ‘F’ grade (below 50% marks) has 0.00 Grade Points and requires mandatory repetition of the course in the immediate junior session. A ‘D’ grade (50–54% marks, 1.00 GP) is a conditional low pass; repeating the course is mandatory only if the student’s cumulative CGPA is below 2.00.
